# Licence and privacy

## Privacy

**Windsock has no accounts, no telemetry and no analytics.** Nothing about you
or your computer is ever sent to the author. There is no server behind
Windsock and no way for the project to learn that you installed it.

Everything Windsock writes stays in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Windsock`: your usage
history, your settings, and rolling logs. Nothing is uploaded, sold or shared.

Address locations on the world map are looked up **offline**, from a database
shipped inside the application. Nothing is sent to any geolocation service.

Windsock reaches the network only to look up the names of addresses you are
already connected to, to trace a route when you ask a graph to draw one, and
to check this project's releases page for a newer version. Each can be turned
off in Settings.

**Windsock installs no kernel driver and contains none.**

The full policy is in `PRIVACY.md`, published at
https://github.com/ShariarShuvo1/Windsock
